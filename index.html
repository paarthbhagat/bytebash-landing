LEVEL 0

About the Game

BYTE BASH is a 24-hour hackathon built like an arcade cabinet: pick your team, drop a coin, and see how far you get before the timer runs out. No genre restrictions — web, mobile, hardware, AI, games. Bring an idea, leave with a working build and a story.

▮▮

Who's it for

Any college student — solo or in teams up to 4. First-time hackers welcome; mentors are on the floor all night.

▮▮▮

What you build

Anything that runs. Web apps, bots, browser games, IoT hacks — judged on execution, not buzzword count.

▮

Why it's different

Live leaderboard, checkpoint judging every 6 hours, and a soundtrack that gets faster as the clock runs down.

LEVEL 5

Rulebook

  • Open to all currently enrolled college students. Teams of 1–4.
  • All code must be written during the 24-hour window. Pre-built assets and open-source libraries are fine — pre-built products aren't.
  • Using AI tools to build is allowed and encouraged. Be ready to explain what you built and how.
  • Every team must demo live at the end. No pre-recorded submissions.
  • Be excellent to each other. Harassment of any kind ends your run — no exceptions.

LEVEL 6

Frequently Asked

Do I need a team to register?

No — solo registration is open. We run a team-formation mixer during check-in for anyone who wants to squad up.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation is free. Meals, snacks, and a place to crash for the night are provided.

What should I bring?

Laptop, charger, college ID, and anything that keeps you awake past 3am.

Can we use AI tools during the hackathon?

Yes. Just be ready to explain your architecture and decisions to the judges — using AI well is part of the skill we're evaluating.

How are teams judged?

On execution, originality, technical depth, and how well the demo actually works — not on how many features you crammed in.
